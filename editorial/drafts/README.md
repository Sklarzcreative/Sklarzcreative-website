# Drafts — originals, unmodified

Every draft that goes through the Editorial Director lands here first, and is
**never edited afterwards**. Not by the agent, not to fix a typo, not to tidy
formatting.

The reason is comparison. An editor can make a piece technically cleaner and
less interesting, and the only way to catch that is to read the original against
the edit, line by line. A draft that has been quietly improved in place cannot
do that job any more.

If you want to revise your own draft, that is a new draft — `<slug>-v2.md` —
not an edit to this one.

## Naming

`<slug>.md` — lowercase, hyphenated, matching the article's eventual URL slug
where one is known.

Front matter is optional. When present, the agent reads `title`, `imprint`,
`author` and `status`.

```yaml
---
title: AI Is Making Execution Cheap. Judgment Is Becoming More Valuable.
imprint: Curves Ahead
author: Cassandra Sklarz
status: draft
---
```

Reviews of these drafts are written to `../reviews/`.
