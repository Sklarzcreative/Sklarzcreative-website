## Community publishing core principle

**Community publishing is not link distribution.** Contribute useful thinking
first. Let profile discovery happen second.

Every community adaptation should feel native to where it appears.

### Reddit

- **Strategic role:** discovery, discussion, market intelligence, idea testing,
  objections and audience language.
- **Priority topics:** marketing, branding, entrepreneurship, small business, AI,
  technology, creators, startups, science communication, biotech, media
  production, podcasting, documentary, cannabis/hemp industry,
  Massachusetts/local topics.
- **Preferred format:** 250–700 words.
- **Structure:** Observation → reasoning → example → question.
- **Avoid:** dropping newsletter links without context, copying identical posts
  into multiple subreddits, promotional intros, leading with credentials,
  mentioning Sklarz Creative unless relevant.
- **CTA:** usually a genuine question, not a link.

### LinkedIn groups

- **Strategic role:** professional authority and relationships.
- **Topics:** brand strategy, marketing, emerging technology, business strategy,
  entrepreneurship, science communication, media, industry analysis.
- **Preferred format:** 200–500 words.
- **Tone:** professional but conversational.
- **CTA:** invite practitioners to compare experience.

### Facebook groups

- **Strategic role:** small-business discovery, local networking, peer
  relationships, women-in-business communities, industry discussion.
- **Priority groups:** Worcester-area business, Massachusetts entrepreneurs,
  Massachusetts small business, women business owners, marketing groups, creator
  groups, wellness business groups, industry communities.
- **Preferred format:** 150–500 words.
- **Strong material:** lessons learned, questions, business experiments,
  frameworks, tool comparisons, local business issues, website ownership,
  marketing prioritization.

### Substack Notes

- **Strategic role:** discovery inside Substack.
- **Use for:** one insight from an article, short observations, questions,
  research findings, quotes from own publication, reactions to developments,
  commentary on other writers.
- **Preferred length:** one paragraph to ~250 words.
- **Do not** let Notes become a link feed.

### Sklarz Creative Discord

**Strategic role:** owned community and reader feedback.

Recurring written properties:

- **The Question** — one strategic discussion each week
- **Worth Watching** — development, platform change, research item, campaign or
  trend
- **Field Notes** — short observation from current work/research
- **Research Drop** — paper, report, dataset or resource plus explanation
- **The Breakdown** — analysis of one company, trend, campaign, platform or
  communication problem
- **Community Brief** — roundup of strongest discussions

## Community lanes and their best adaptations

**AI / technology communities.** Best subjects: workflow design, tool
proliferation, automation, productivity, adoption, human judgment, AI-generated
sameness, business implementation, small-business technology. Best adaptations:
AI Is Making Execution Cheap; Businesses Keep Buying AI Tools Before Fixing
Workflows; Automation Can Make a Bad Process Faster; The Hidden Cost of Adding
Another Tool; What Happens When Everyone Can Create Content Instantly?

**Marketing / branding communities.** Best subjects: positioning, messaging,
trust, differentiation, content strategy, premium positioning, consumer
behavior, growth, brand systems. Best adaptations: The Brand Problem Hiding
Behind Your Content Problem; Premium Design Can't Fix Weak Positioning; Every
Piece of Content Needs One Job; The Four Questions to Ask Before Publishing;
Clarity Is a Growth Strategy; The Cost of Being Indistinguishable.

**Entrepreneur / small business communities.** Best subjects: systems, software
costs, website ownership, platform overload, prioritization, growth decisions,
automation, outsourcing, founder operations. Best adaptations: Why Every
Business Doesn't Need Another Platform; Why Small Businesses Should Own Their
Websites; Building Systems Before You Have a Team; The Difference Between Saving
Money and Creating Operational Drag; Why I Stopped Treating Every New Platform
as an Opportunity.

**Creator / media communities.** Best subjects: audience ownership, newsletters,
podcasts, documentary, short-form vs long-form, repurposing, creator businesses,
media economics. Best adaptations: Creators Are Becoming Media Companies; Why
Newsletters Are Becoming Interesting Again; Why Depth May Become More Valuable
as Short-Form Becomes Infinite; One Idea, Eighteen Pieces of Content; What
Documentary Storytelling Can Teach Brands.

**Science communication communities.** Best subjects: scientific accuracy,
evidence, consumer understanding, technical communication, research translation,
founder communication, emerging industries. Best adaptations: Scientific
Accuracy and Good Marketing Are Not Opposites; Why Emerging Industries Have a
Communication Problem; Why Evidence Builds Brands Differently Than Hype; When
Innovation Moves Faster Than Public Understanding; Why Technical Founders
Struggle to Explain Their Best Ideas.

**Cannabis / hemp communities.** Strategic role: industry credibility and
relationships without making the broader publication dependent on one industry.
Best subjects: research, biotechnology, genetics, consumer education, science
communication, regulation/messaging, product differentiation, trust, stigma,
media representation. **Rule:** the subject should be substantive enough to
belong in the community even if Sklarz Creative did not exist.

**Massachusetts / Worcester communities.** Strategic role: local network
development and potential business relationships. Best subjects: small-business
technology, local entrepreneurship, marketing, community visibility, website
ownership, events, regional creative economy, practical business resources.
Avoid turning local groups into continuous business advertising.

## Community adaptation ladder

| Format | Length |
| --- | --- |
| Newsletter | 800–1,500+ words |
| LinkedIn post | 350–700 words |
| Forum post | 250–700 words |
| Group discussion | 150–400 words |
| Micro discussion | 50–200 words |
| Poll | One question with 3–4 meaningful options |
| Community follow-up | Summarize what people disagreed about or added |

## Feedback loop

Every substantive community post should produce at least one of:

1. New question
2. Objection
3. Example
4. Terminology
5. Future article idea

Record those insights in the editorial backlog.

Publishing system: **Research → Publish → Discuss → Learn → Publish better.**

## Operating rule

Research the idea once. Transform it many times. Do not maintain separate
intellectual strategies for every platform.

## Important human-voice rule

**Do not fabricate premises** such as "I spent the week investigating this"
unless Cassandra actually did. Use true language such as "I've been looking
into…," "Something I've been noticing…," "I keep seeing…," "I've been testing…,"
or "After comparing…" only when supported by actual work.
