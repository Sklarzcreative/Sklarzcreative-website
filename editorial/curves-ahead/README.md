## Two rules from the handoff that bind the agent

**AI is production infrastructure, not the reader-facing brand.** Reader-facing
publication copy does not mention AI-assisted writing *unless AI is
substantively relevant to the subject*. Edition 01 is about AI as a subject —
that is the publication analysing a shift, and it is entirely in scope. What
stays out is any account of how the writing itself was produced.

**Never fabricate a premise.** From the backlog document: do not write "I spent
the week investigating this" unless she did. "I've been looking into…",
"Something I've been noticing…", "I keep seeing…", "I've been testing…" are
true-language alternatives — but only when actual work supports them.
