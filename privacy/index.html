How this site handles data

Privacy

Short, because there is not much to say. This site has no analytics, no advertising, no cookies of its own, and no third-party trackers. The one place it collects anything is the Trust-First Content Scorecard, and only if you choose to fill it in.

What is collected

Only what you type into the Scorecard.

If you submit the form on the Trust-First Content Scorecard, Sklarz Creative records:

  • First name
  • Email address
  • Whether you opted in to the follow-up series

Alongside that, three technical values are stored so the tool can be maintained and abuse can be filtered: which resource and page the submission came from, how long the form was open before it was submitted (a spam signal — automated submissions arrive impossibly fast), and any campaign parameters present in the link you arrived through, such as utm_source. Nothing here identifies a device or follows you between sites.

If you complete the twenty statements, your five category scores, your total, and your lowest-scoring signal are recorded against that submission. That is the whole point of the follow-up series: advice about the weakest signal is only useful if it knows which one it is.

Your answers stay on your device while you work. Scoring runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent while you move through the statements.

If you complete the scorecard without giving an email, the five scores and the total are still recorded — with no name, no email, and no identifier of any kind attached. That exists for one reason: to know what proportion of people who open the tool actually finish it. It cannot be traced to you, to your device, or to any other visit, and it is not used for marketing. If you would rather it were not recorded at all, say so and the behaviour will be switched off.

Why, and where it lives

To see whether the tool is worth building on.

Two reasons, and no others. To know whether the Scorecard is being used and deserves further work. And, only if you ticked the box, to send you the short Trust-First follow-up series.

Submissions are stored in a private Google Sheet inside the Sklarz Creative Google Workspace account. They are not sold, rented, shared, published, or passed to any advertising network. If an email platform is used to send the follow-up series, your name and address are shared with that platform for that purpose alone — and only if you opted in.

The Scorecard stores one flag in your browser’s local storage so it does not ask you twice on the same device. That is not a cookie, it is never sent anywhere, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Third parties

What else your browser talks to.

This site loads no tracking scripts. Two outside services are involved, and both are visible in what you do:

Google Fonts

The three typefaces are served by Google Fonts, which means your browser requests them from Google and Google sees the request.

Calendly

Booking a discovery call takes you to Calendly, which has its own privacy terms. Nothing is shared with Calendly unless you go there.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub processes the requests needed to serve pages to you.

Your choices

Unsubscribe, or ask to be deleted.

The follow-up series is opt-in and unchecked by default. You can use the Scorecard in full without agreeing to any email. Every message in the series carries an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing stops the series immediately.

To see what is held about you, correct it, or have it deleted, email sklarzcreative@gmail.com and say so. No particular wording is needed. Deletion means the row is removed from the sheet and the address is removed from the email platform.

Records are kept while the Scorecard is in use and there is a reason to keep them. Anything no longer serving either purpose is deleted.
