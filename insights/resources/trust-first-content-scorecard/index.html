Turn the score into a decision

Do not fix everything at once.

Start with the lowest category that matters most to the customer decision you are trying to support.

1. Lowest category
2. One change to make in the next 7 days
3. One piece of proof or context to add
4. One destination or CTA to fix

Recheck before publishing: What is the point? Who is it for? What should they think or do afterward? Where does it lead next? What is the one primary job?
