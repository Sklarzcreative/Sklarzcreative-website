Trust-First Content Scorecard

Score one real customer-facing touchpoint across five signals: clarity, consistency, credibility, connection, and conversion. The goal is not a perfect number. It is finding the weakest signal and deciding what to change next.

How to use it

One touchpoint. Twenty decisions.

Choose one homepage, landing page, social profile, campaign, email, sales page, or piece of content. Score each statement based on what is actually present, not what the brand intends to communicate.

  • 0 = No / missing
  • 1 = Partly / inconsistent
  • 2 = Yes / clearly

Four of the five signals scored here — clarity, consistency, credibility, and connection — come from the trust lens introduced in File 001 of The Trust Files. Conversion is added because a score is only useful if it changes what happens next.

Turn the score into a decision

Do not fix everything at once.

Start with the lowest category that matters most to the customer decision you are trying to support.

1. Lowest category
2. One change to make in the next 7 days
3. One piece of proof or context to add
4. One destination or CTA to fix

Recheck before publishing: What is the point? Who is it for? What should they think or do afterward? Where does it lead next? What is the one primary job?
