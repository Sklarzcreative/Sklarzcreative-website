Evidence & Context

Research Notes

Short, source-conscious examinations of useful evidence, emerging ideas, and claims that deserve a closer look. Research Notes distinguish what is known, what is suggested, and what remains uncertain.

Editorial Standard

Evidence before certainty.

Research Notes are not designed to make weak evidence sound exciting. Scientific, legal, market, and behavioral claims are separated from interpretation and opinion. Where evidence is early, mixed, indirect, or incomplete, the language should say so.

1. Question

Start with one clear claim or problem worth examining.

2. Evidence

Prioritize primary and authoritative sources.

3. Limits

Identify uncertainty, missing context, and competing explanations.

4. Implication

Explain what the evidence reasonably changes for a brand, founder, or audience.
