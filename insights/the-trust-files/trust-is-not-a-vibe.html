The Trust Files · File 001

Trust Is Not a Vibe

What actually makes a brand trustworthy? The first file examines trust through four practical dimensions: clarity, consistency, credibility, and connection.

Episode in production

Hosted by Cassandra Sklarz · Sklarz Creative

Trust is not something a company can declare. It is the conclusion people reach after comparing what a brand says with what it repeatedly does.

The premise

A polished identity can influence perception, but it is not proof. Trust grows when communication, evidence, behavior, and experience reinforce one another over time.

ClarityCan people understand what you do, who it is for, and why it matters?
ConsistencyDo your promises, behavior, and customer experience agree across touchpoints?
CredibilityAre your claims supported by evidence, experience, transparency, or demonstrated results?
ConnectionDo people feel respected, understood, and treated like participants rather than targets?

What the episode covers

File 001 explores why trust is an outcome rather than a slogan, how contradictions weaken confidence, why responsible communication matters in science-driven and trust-sensitive industries, and how founders can examine the signals their brand is already sending.

A five-touchpoint trust audit

Choose your homepage, social bio, most recent post, inquiry or sales process, and customer follow-up.

For each one, ask: Is the message clear? Does it match the others? Are the claims supported? Does the audience feel respected? Is the next step obvious?
