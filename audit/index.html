Common questions

Before you book

Is this an SEO audit?

No. Search and AI discoverability are part of the review, but the Mini Audit is broader. It looks at whether your public presence is clear, credible, consistent, easy to find, and easy to act on.

Do I need to be planning a redesign?

No. The audit is useful before a redesign, campaign, launch, or larger marketing investment because it helps determine what actually needs to change first.

Will you implement the recommendations?

Implementation is separate. You can handle the action plan internally, give it to an existing partner, or ask Sklarz Creative for a separate scope. Website strategy and builds are available when deeper website work is justified.

Who is this best for?

Founder-led, expert-led, wellness, science-informed, purpose-driven, and emerging businesses are a strong fit, especially when the offer is complex or trust matters heavily in the buying decision.
