Founder Biography

Cassandra Sklarz

Cassandra Sklarz is a strategic marketing consultant, creative director, storyteller, researcher, and founder of Sklarz Creative. She helps innovative and purpose-driven organizations build credibility through clear positioning, authentic storytelling, thoughtful content, and meaningful audience connection.

Her experience spans brand strategy, social media, content creation, event promotion, community engagement, partnerships, podcast and media production, scientific research, and business development.

Cassandra is particularly interested in wellness, biotechnology, health innovation, botanical science, emerging technologies, entrepreneurship, and companies translating complex ideas into meaningful products and services.

“Trust is the most valuable marketing asset a company can build.”

Through Sklarz Creative, she works with founders, organizations, creators, and innovative brands that want more than visibility. They want to be understood, remembered, and believed.
