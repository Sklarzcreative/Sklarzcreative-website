Founder biography

Cassandra Sklarz

Cassandra Sklarz is a strategic marketing consultant, creative director, storyteller, researcher, and founder of Sklarz Creative. She helps innovative and purpose-driven organisations build credibility through clearer positioning, responsible storytelling, thoughtful content, and meaningful audience connection.

Her work spans brand strategy, social media, content creation, event promotion, community engagement, partnerships, podcast and media production, research, and business development.

Cassandra is particularly interested in science-driven and trust-sensitive categories, emerging technology, entrepreneurship, wellness innovation, and organisations translating complex ideas into useful products, services, or public understanding.

Trust is the most valuable marketing asset a company can build.

Through Sklarz Creative, she works with founders, organisations, creators, and innovative brands that want more than visibility. They want to be understood, remembered, and believed.
