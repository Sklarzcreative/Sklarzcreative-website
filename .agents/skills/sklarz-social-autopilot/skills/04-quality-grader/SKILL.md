# Skill: Quality Grader

## Purpose

Evaluate content against the correct objective, not merely "virality."

## Sklarz Creative score

Score 100 total:
- Strategic relevance: 20
- Value / useful insight: 20
- Brand voice: 15
- Credibility / accuracy: 15
- Specificity: 10
- Platform fit: 10
- Hook: 5
- CTA: 5

Target: 82+

## Curves Ahead score

Score 100 total:
- Factual accuracy: 25
- Original analysis: 20
- Usefulness: 15
- Source quality: 10
- Specificity: 10
- Voice: 10
- Hook: 5
- CTA / next step: 5

Target: 85+

## Our Family Story review

Use qualitative review: source fidelity, privacy, consent/preapproval, respectful tone and no invented experiences. A numeric score never authorizes a family post. Sensitive material goes to Cass regardless of writing quality.

## Automatic failure conditions

A post cannot pass if it:
- invents a fact or source
- misstates a product capability
- presents speculation as fact
- contains an unsupported scientific/medical claim
- violates a known brand guardrail
- contains confidential/private material
- creates legal/reputational risk that requires human review
- lacks enough context to support its main claim

## Revision loop

If below threshold:
1. identify the weakest 2-3 dimensions
2. revise only what is needed
3. preserve strong material
4. re-grade
5. stop after 3 loops and flag for human editorial review if still below target

## Final artifact gate

Apply this review after repurposing and media selection to each final caption, thread, Note, script and visual text. Inspect link/destination accuracy and platform/media fit. Thresholds are editorial heuristics, not factual evidence or approval. Recheck after substantive changes. No health/scientific claim or other review-required item becomes autonomous by scoring well.
