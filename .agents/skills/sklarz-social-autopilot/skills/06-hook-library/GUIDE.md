# Skill: Hook Library

## Purpose

Strengthen openings without forcing clickbait or a fake "viral" voice.

## Preferred hook families

1. Specific observation
   - "This week, [company] did something more important than another product launch."

2. Consequence
   - "[Change] matters because it removes [old constraint]."

3. Useful tension
   - "[Specific change] removes [constraint], leaving [practical next question]."

4. Data/detail
   - "[Number/date/product detail] changes the economics of [activity]."

5. Question
   - "What happens when [new capability] becomes ordinary infrastructure?"

6. Experience/lesson
   - "The more I build with AI tools, the more obvious one bottleneck becomes."

7. Client implication
   - "If you're a small team, this matters for one practical reason."

8. Contrarian, sparingly
   - only when the evidence genuinely supports the contrast

## Avoid

- "Stop scrolling"
- "This changes everything" without proof
- fake secrets
- fake controversy
- "Everyone is doing X wrong"
- "Nobody is talking about this" unless demonstrably true
- repetitive "X isn't the future. It is the present."

Use first-person experience hooks only when Cass supplied that experience. Do not invent a story to fit a template. Vary openings; templates are optional and should not create repeated formulas.
