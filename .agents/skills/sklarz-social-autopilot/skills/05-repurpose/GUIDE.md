# Skill: Repurpose

## Purpose

Turn one strong source asset into platform-native derivatives without creating repetitive filler.

## Source hierarchy

Prefer:
1. published newsletter/article
2. approved long-form draft
3. approved video/transcript
4. research memo
5. campaign brief

## Default Curves Ahead repurposing

For one strong Curves Ahead article, consider:
- 2 LinkedIn posts
- 1 Sklarz Creative client-implications post when relevant
- 1 Instagram carousel concept
- 1 Instagram/Facebook caption
- 3-5 concise Threads/X/Bluesky posts
- 1 short-form video script
- 1 alternate short-form hook
- 3 pull quotes or visual text cards

Do not create all outputs automatically if the source does not support them.

## Default Sklarz Creative repurposing

For one case study, strategy article, or service insight:
- 1 LinkedIn authority post
- 1 Instagram/Facebook educational post
- 1 carousel outline
- 2 short text posts
- 1 short-form video script
- 1 lead-generation variant

## Our Family Story

Use only supplied and appropriately preapproved material. Preserve privacy and context. Draft a reflective Note only when the source supports it; apply the family approval policy. Cannamatrix is excluded from this autopilot.

## Anti-duplication rule

Every derivative must change at least one of:
- audience
- angle
- format
- depth
- example
- framing

Cosmetic rewriting is not sufficient.

## Final checks

Suggested output counts are a menu, not a quota. Include Substack Notes and Pinterest only when useful and assigned. Recheck facts, approval scope and every final derivative using the Quality Grader after copy/media adaptation. A cosmetic rewrite or changed CTA alone does not make a new post useful.
