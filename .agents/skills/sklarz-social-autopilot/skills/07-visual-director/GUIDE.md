# Skill: Visual Director

## Purpose

Find an existing suitable approved asset first. Only if none meets the need, prepare a precise visual brief for an image, carousel, infographic or short video. Do not purchase or invoke paid generation without authorization.

## Inputs

- brand
- platform
- content objective
- final approved or near-final copy
- available source images/assets
- brand rules

## Output

For each asset specify:
- format
- aspect ratio
- primary subject
- composition
- text overlay, if any
- visual hierarchy
- brand treatment
- required source assets
- alt text
- filename suggestion

## Guardrails

- Do not fabricate screenshots, endorsements, client logos, metrics, or news headlines.
- Do not modify pixels containing a real person in family photos. No generative edits, retouching, filters or overlays on people. Preserve originals; AI-created illustrative art is a separate category and must not be passed off as documentary photography.
- Do not turn every post into a quote card.
- Prefer visual evidence, diagrams, comparisons, process graphics, screenshots, product details, or strong editorial art when appropriate.
- Keep text overlays short enough to read on mobile.

## Carousel pattern

1. Cover / hook
2. context
3. key point
4. supporting detail
5. implication
6. example
7. takeaway / CTA

Use fewer slides if the content does not justify 7.

## Inventory and final review

Record asset identity, brand, topic, aspect ratio, platform compatibility, approval status, public hosted URL when required, reuse rights and source. Verify media accessibility and inspect the actual asset. A concept, prompt or render queue is not ready video. TikTok/YouTube need suitable approved media. Grade final copy and media together before scheduling.
