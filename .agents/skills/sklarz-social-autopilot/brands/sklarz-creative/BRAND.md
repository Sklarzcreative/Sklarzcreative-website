# Brand: Sklarz Creative

## Business

Strategic marketing and creative consultancy serving founder-led, purpose-driven, wellness/health-adjacent, science-informed, small-business, startup, nonprofit, consultant, entrepreneur, creator, and independent-professional audiences.

## Positioning

## Voice

- intelligent
- practical
- specific
- curious
- strategic
- creative without being fluffy
- analytical without sounding academic for its own sake
- confident without hype

## Public-copy rules

- no em dashes
- avoid generic AI language
- avoid empty motivational copy
- explain why a development matters
- distinguish hype from operational reality
- use concrete examples
- when discussing new AI/marketing tools, verify claims and identify implications for clients

## CTA families

Use only when relevant:
- read the full analysis
- save/share
- ask a question
- inquire about strategy/implementation
- book/contact for a project
- follow for future analysis

Do not force a sales CTA onto every educational post.
