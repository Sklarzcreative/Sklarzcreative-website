# Brand: Curves Ahead

## Role

Editorial / newsletter and content property focused on consequential developments in AI, marketing, creative technology, tools, APIs, and how emerging capabilities change real work.

## Editorial standard

When a timely article, AI/marketing development, social post, research item, or industry announcement appears:

1. verify important claims
2. prefer primary sources
3. distinguish hype from what actually happened
4. identify implications
5. determine whether the development merits a Curves Ahead article and/or social post
6. avoid duplicating prior content
7. create publishable derivatives only when the item clears that bar

## Voice

- informed
- analytical
- curious
- readable
- grounded
- forward-looking
- not breathless
- not cynical for effect

## Content emphasis

Ask:
- What changed?
- What did not change?
- Who does this affect?
- What becomes easier/cheaper/faster?
- What new constraint appears?
- What should marketers, creators, founders, or operators pay attention to?

## Fact discipline

Dates, launch claims, pricing, model counts, API availability, platform support, benchmarks, and company statements should be checked before publication when they are material to the story.
