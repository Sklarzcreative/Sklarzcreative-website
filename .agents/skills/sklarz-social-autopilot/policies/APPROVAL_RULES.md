# Approval rules

## Existing authorization

Cass has already authorized safe evergreen marketing education for autonomous scheduling: brand/content strategy, trust/discoverability, approved positioning, service education without new claims, approved Curves Ahead derivatives, and low-risk tool observations after factual verification. Do not ask again for this class solely because this pack is installed. Quality scores alone never establish authorization.

Retain approval provenance on each item: explicit approval or standing authorization class, scope, source and relevant date. Changed claims, context, account or media require reassessment. Unknown classification defaults to REVIEW. HOLD means do not schedule or release it.

## Cass review required

- Sensitive family information, children's private information, private family details.
- Current legal/court/DCF matters, allegations, identifiable conflict or disputes.
- Fundraising or legal factual claims.
- Client names/work, confidential information, client-sensitive work or performance/results claims.
- Health/scientific claims, new pricing, major announcements, controversial personal material, or anything materially uncertain.
- New sponsored/partner claims or new offers outside existing approval.

Fact-checking does not replace required human review. An old approved post does not authorize altered sensitive claims.

## Our Family Story

Ordinary memories, neutral reflections, everyday family life and appropriate historical/archival stories can be prepared. Auto-schedule only when the particular material or a clearly defined batch/category has been preapproved; being on the same profile as professional Notes does not supply approval. Never invent first-person experiences or private details. Sensitive content always returns to Cass.

Do not modify pixels containing a real person. Layout and typography must preserve those pixels, including avoiding overlays on the person. Do not use generative editing, retouching or filters on people in family photos. AI-created artwork is a separate category and must not be represented as documentary photography.

## Scope and states

Cannamatrix remains separate and is not auto-routed by this pack, even if a topic seems relevant. A separate, explicit, in-scope request is required for Cannamatrix work.

Use provider-supported draft/approval states and the existing record schema. Conceptual states: DRAFT, REVIEW, APPROVED, HOLD. These are not assumed provider API enum values. Never bulk-release historical HOLD rows or mark a row approved merely to make a publisher run.
