# Repurposing matrix

| Source | Useful derivatives | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Approved Curves Ahead article | Personal LinkedIn analysis, X/Threads insight, Substack Note, Sklarz carousel or client implication | Newsletter article remains native; verify current claims |
| Sklarz strategy source | Educational social post, carousel, Pinterest derivative, professional Note | Match assigned route and current capacity |
| Approved family story | Reflective Cassandra Substack Note | Apply family preapproval/privacy gate; never Sklarz Metricool |
| Approved video/transcript | Suitable clip, text insight, carousel | Verify reuse rights and actual ready media |
| Client work | Only explicitly approved adaptations | No invented permissions, names or results |

Cannamatrix is excluded. One canonical source governs facts. Each derivative needs a distinct audience, angle, format, depth or useful example; merely swapping words or a CTA is insufficient. Choose only formats supported by the source and plan capacity. Grade every final derivative after adaptation.
